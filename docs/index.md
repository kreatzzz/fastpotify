  <h2 id="landing-signal-title">A player with a pulse, not a pile of tabs.</h2>
  <p>Woofer keeps the useful parts of a music player in one calm window: your library, the queue, lyrics, and the devices already on your account. It starts quickly, stays out of the way, and tells you when Spotify needs something from you.</p>
  <div class="landing-signals">
    <div class="landing-signal">
      <strong>play here / play there</strong>
      <span>Move playback to a speaker, phone, TV, or another computer without losing the thread.</span>
    </div>
    <div class="landing-signal">
      <strong>read between the lines</strong>
      <span>Follow synced lyrics, echo a translation, or romanize a script for a better sing-along.</span>
    </div>
    <div class="landing-signal">
      <strong>make it yours</strong>
      <span>Light or dark, album-art colour, shortcuts, a tray, and a Winamp mini player when the mood calls for it.</span>

  </div>
